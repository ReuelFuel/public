A Shining Light

The Bible is a special book. Over 40 authors wrote it over a 1,500-year period, and it is filled with history, prophecy (future events), and guidance on how we can be saved and live in this present world. Simply put, it is how God communicates with us.

A common metaphor used for the Bible is light ''Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path’ (Psalms 119:105). By studying it, we gain a clearer view of God's character and His purpose for us. This not only gives us hope and comfort but also helps us understand the value and purpose God sees in each individual. By applying the lessons given in the Bible, we are transformed, becoming more like Him who speaks to us through its pages. As it says, 'The path of the righteous is like the morning sun, shining ever brighter till the full light of day. ('Proverbs 4:18.)
